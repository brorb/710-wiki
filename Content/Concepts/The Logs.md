The Logs are videos uploaded to the [[710 Tone|7/10 Tone]] channel that usually contain red and black blocks (also known as packets of data, see [[LOG-120]]) with some soft noise in the background and a counter at the end that counts down (which is presumably the [[Station Timer]]). These videos usually have the word "LOG", a number, and a file extension (.mp4) in the title (although the file extension labels are slightly discontinued in 2026), however not all logs are labeled like this (for example, [[33LOG-13]] and [[machina_ad_mortem.mp4]]).

According to the [[Hello]] video, the owner of the [[710 Tone|7/10 Tone]] channel was told to create the program that makes these logs by an undisclosed group of people and it is designed to pull data from [[Caper Waves]]. This program is officially dubbed the "7/10" program in [[LOG-1.mp4]], presumably running on [[LINX-OS]].

[[TTH]] are in charge of making them, which was hinted by [[INDI]] in [[STATION_8 TERMINAL 8]] and then officially confirmed in [[LOG-120]]. They are also meant to be deciphered like some kind of data (also confirmed by [[INDI]] in [[STATION_8 TERMINAL 8]]), however they are too complex to be properly decoded without proper tools or skills. Most likely they are meant to be decoded through a special program called a DRP (Data Reporting Program), however this has yet to be confirmed as if 1st May 2026. According to [[INDI]], they are important to "someone else", but he didn't exactly specify who it was. [^1] However, judging by what the presentation shown in [[LOG-120]] stated, it is most likely important to [[He|HIM]] or another entity.

[[TTH]] and [[He|HIM]] exchange Logs with each other to send data from [[Caper Waves]], or more specifically a special type of Caper Wave called the 7/10 tone (hence [[710 Tone|the name of the YouTube channel]]).

They are not originally meant to be seen by the viewers[^2], but as for why they remain public on the YouTube channel is unknown (aside from getting purged sometime after July 10th).

At the start of each log, there is a screen that lists certain values for its data: Port Call, Log, System, and System ID. Red and black squares dance around the screen for the majority of the video, the red squares represent data that relates to something inside of [[He|HIS]] complex, while the black squares represent discarded data packets. Each frame of a Log is a snapshot of 1 logged type of data.

In 2025, the program was updated in [[update log 001]], and therefore the look of the screen and data has since changed starting from [[LOG-008.mp4]].
## Oddities

Not all LOGs appear "normal" at first, sometimes strange occurrences happen within some of them, such as [[LOG-27.mp4]] or [[LOG-95.mp4]]. These oddities might be of some significance to the lore. The end counter also has oddities such as after every 10 increments in the counter, it scrolls through numbers as its counting down by 1 point. Other times it repeats itself between logs or glitches out.
