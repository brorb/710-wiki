An operating system that appeared in [[710 Tone|7/10 Tone]] videos, where the program in charge of creating [[The Logs]] is installed (officially called the "7/10" program). It seems to be a kernel-based operating system with multiple terminals (so far only Terminal 8 and Terminal 10 are known to exist).
