[YouTube link here](https://www.youtube.com/@710NPP)

Second channel associated with [[710 Tone|7/10 Tone]], created 31 January 2025. Before [[Travis]] died, the channel was controlled by [[Content/Characters/INDI]] during the [[STATION_8 TRIM 5]] livestream, until [[Travis]] gave control of the main channel ([[710 Tone|7/10 Tone]]) over to [[Content/Characters/INDI]]. During [[STATION_8 TRIM 90]] and [[STATION_8 TERMINAL 8]], [[Kepler]] was managing the 7/10 NPP channel.

Usually the channel's uploads correspond to certain videos from [[710 Tone|7/10 Tone]], such as [[20240NA@ 25109]] and [[12_capturedRecall 011]], with [[0001 Bin]] being the only unique video so far.

In November 2025, the channel was completely purged of all three videos, most likely in response to the "ERROR 9999+" [[Community Posts|Community Post]] that also resulted in [[710 Tone|7/10 Tone]]'s purge.
