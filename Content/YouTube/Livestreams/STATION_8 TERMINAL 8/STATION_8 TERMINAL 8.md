**STATION_8 TERMINAL 8 Livestream Chat Summary**

## Chat Summary

Livestream proceeds [[STATION_8 TRIM 90]]. Technical difficulties ([[Station Interruption]]) immediately as it starts, which get fixed by [[INDI]]. Stream resumes as normal into the next following day (8th July), viewers point out that there is only two more days left until the [[Station Timer]] ends, to which [[INDI]] says "ONLY 2 DAYS LEFT? WHY DOES IT ALWAYS HAVE TO END LIKE THIS?" in response.

As the stream continues normally with the [[Station Timer]] counting down, a couple of viewers try to get [[Long String]] to work in the chat to no avail. [[INDI]] tells them to stop, saying that it's not going to work, then one of the viewers asks what [[Long String]] is, to which [[INDI]] simply replies with "A TOOL." Viewer asks what [[Long String]]'s use is for, but [[Kepler]] ([[710 NPP|7/10 NPP]]) quickly steps in to stop the conversation from progressing any further, saying "It's a tool used by us now quit talking about it."

[[INDI]] laments about how he just wanted to get rid of the security and that he didn't mean to hurt it, then [[Kepler]] tries to reassure him that it wasn't his fault and that it was "their lack of intelligence" that killed it. After a short while, [[INDI]] creates a moderator for the stream chat named SYSTEMterminal8 (see [[SYSTEM]]), which has the SYSTEM ID of 478829 and it pulls the livestream values as "439". [[Kepler]] asks what the mod is (as if unaware of how livestream moderation works), [[INDI]] explains to him that it is a moderator, then [[Kepler]] asks if they really need something like that, to which [[INDI]] replies that he doesn't know. The moderator leaks some messages from the [[Private Room]], then [[Kepler]] panics and tells [[INDI]] to turn it off, then [[INDI]] disconnects the mod.

After a moment, [[INDI]] says that he fixed the mod, saying that it's "not going to pull from anywhere important", then the mod reconnects, returning the livestream's values as "FALSE". Kepler expresses his approval of this, suggesting that the "live values" are actually confidential information that shouldn't have been public anyway. However, after a few moments the moderator detects some malicious comments (possibly from the stream chat, one of viewers mentioned [[TTH]] and [[REN]] which was probably what set it off) and sends an R-5 request but gets denied. [[Kepler]] asks what an R-5 request is, to which [[INDI]] explains that it is a protocol to inform deletion of the entire livestream, but he luckily denied it. [[Kepler]] suggests to [[INDI]] that he turn off the mod again, then [[INDI]] disconnects the mod. After a little while, [[Kepler]] tells [[INDI]] to stop putting their "entries" (most likely the [[Private Room]]) into somewhere, when [[INDI]] asks why, [[Kepler]] says "it's not working for that.", then [[INDI]] says that he'll find a "different method."

Stream proceeds as normal until the moderator eventually reconnects on its own and leaks the logged-in accounts of the Private Room: IN_DI.servertype, K3P13R, SYSTEMterminal8, and data_messagesystem05. It disconnects right after doing this, then a furious [[Kepler]] eventually comes into the stream chat and tells [[INDI]] that the mod logged into the [[Private Room]] and then disconnected, to which [[INDI]] seems confused as to how it did such a thing.

Stream proceeds again as normal before [[INDI]] comes back into the chat to say that him and [[Kepler]] are the only two working with the signals and nobody else, with "one of the moderators" might also have access. A viewer named "The Camera Eye" (who is the main archiver of 7/10 Tone and one of [[The Sleuths]]) playfully says that she's been found out and asks [[INDI]] how he knew of such a thing, to which [[INDI]] says that he doesn't know anything except that the current livestream has 149 views, 7 likes, and 2 concurrent viewers from the analytics tab. The Camera Eye asks if he's talking about the analytics within the YouTube Studio, to which INDI responds with "STUDIO.YOUTUBE.COM AND SOMETHING ELSE AFTER THAT THAT TRAVIS HID FROM ME", confirming that [[Travis]] restricted some control of the channel from [[INDI]]. Then The Camera Eye asks if [[Travis]] is dead and if he was infected with the [[Black Mold]], [[INDI]] replies that he doesn't know, then The Camera Eye follows up with a question about if [[Travis]] could possibly still be alive, but [[INDI]] cuts the conversation short by saying that he can't continue with the topic.

Stream continues as normal until the moderator reconnects, pinging a "SYSTEM_8902 CALLSIGN 5", then it prints out a message one letter at a time that spells out "HELLO" before running an "infect" command. [[INDI]] notices the strange activity and asks who it is that's controlling the mod, the hacker responses with "Yu know xactly Wh I am", however [[INDI]] doesn't recognize who it is right away but he *does* know that he has to delete them. The hacker says that [[INDI]] isn't going to do that, [[INDI]] asks why, then the hacker asks what signal terminal he is in, possibly to either take it over or destroy it ("I love this little thing you got going here"). [[INDI]] suddenly recognizes the hacker as the mysterious "[[He]]" ("WAIT, YOUR NOT HIM ARE YOU?"), then a [[Station Interruption]] interferes with the [[Station Timer]]. [[Kepler]] eventually steps in and [[INDI]] tells him that "[[He]]" most likely hacked the mod, [[Kepler]] switches places with [[INDI]] and takes over the [[710 Tone|7/10 Tone]] channel to try to remedy this problem, saying that [[INDI]] is going to help kill the bot. However, [[Kepler]] unfortunately doesn't have much control on his end since [[Travis]] locked a lot of stuff behind the scenes so [[INDI]] wouldn't mess anything up. [[Kepler]] tries to figure out how [[INDI]] managed things with the channel, until he finally gets it working again on his end. Eventually, after things clear up, [[INDI]] comes back (now as [[710 NPP|7/10 NPP]]) and says that he got rid of the bot and [[Kepler]] thanks him. Then [[INDI]] asks if he can get the account back, to which [[Kepler]] says no because [[INDI]] almost made the mistake of getting both of them into trouble with "Him" and that they'll have to discuss more about it in the [[Private Room]].

Stream continues as normal for a few more hours until a viewer tries to ping both the [[710 Tone|7/10 Tone]] and [[710 NPP|7/10 NPP]] channels to get their attention and ask some questions, to which [[Kepler]] eventually asks why he's getting pinged, stating that he shouldn't be getting pinged. The viewer asks [[Kepler]] what happens when the timer hits 0 and if there are any bombs involved, but unfortunately [[Kepler]] can't tell them anything about that, although he can say that there are no bombs. A couple of viewers ask him about two suspicious characters from chat (AmeraM and ZONE specifically), but [[Kepler]] says that he doesn't know who they are, adding that "all who are working under [[REN]] right now are me and [[INDI]]", which basically means that both AmeraM and ZONE are outside actors. Then they ask if [[REN]] are associated with "The Rebels" (who were mentioned sometime in 2024), to which [[Kepler]] says that he can't talk about that either, but he says that "all you need to know is that me, and hopefully [[INDI]], are not the enemies", implying that those who work under [[REN]] are actually the good guys. Then [[Kepler]] signs off, stating that he has more important to deal with than talk to the viewers.

A few hours later, [[INDI]] comes back into the chat (still as [[710 NPP|7/10 NPP]]) tells [[Kepler]] to look behind him, [[Kepler]] asks for what, to which [[INDI]] says "The code book" with normal caps instead of his usual all-caps text, and [[Kepler]] (still as [[710 Tone|7/10 Tone]]) expresses confusion at this anomaly as he asks what happened to his all caps. [[INDI]] says in his usual all-caps text that he doesn't know but "it" is back (this "it" wasn't specified), then [[Kepler]] asks why the [[Private Room]] keeps changing to "this" (which could mean the YouTube Livestream chat), but [[INDI]] doesn't know why it is happening. A viewer asks [[INDI]] what the [[Private Room]] is for, to which [[INDI]] replies that it is his only reliable way of communication with [[Kepler]]. [[Kepler]] yells at [[INDI]] for telling the audience about such sensitive information, [[INDI]] apologizes, then [[Kepler]] orders him to change back to the [[Private Room]].

Another few hours into the stream later, [[INDI]] regains back control of the [[710 Tone|7/10 Tone]] channel and [[Kepler]] warns him not to screw things up again. [[INDI]] tells him that he made his point and that he can now delete things and [[Kepler]] asks how he managed to do so, to which [[INDI]] replies that he "followed [[Kepler]]'s stuff" and that he "just wasn't close enough". [[Kepler]] tells [[INDI]] "fine, just don't delete anything important", then [[INDI]] deletes their livestream chat conversation, adding that "this will be fun" (hinting at a mischievous nature). One of the viewers tells [[INDI]] not to "kill them" (or delete their comments as well), saying that they are [[INDI]]'s "biggest fan (they swear!)", but [[INDI]] reassures them that they are not in relation to [[REN]] and won't be affected.

At around the four hour mark on the [[Station Timer]], a dial tone similar to the one heard whenever the [[Private Room]] tried to connect in [[{ - . }re]] and [[LOG-62.mp4]] plays over the timer for a moment then cuts off abruptly, then Kepler tells INDI not to do that yet, as "they" won't answer until 2000 (these comments would be deleted afterwards). After an hour of waiting, Kepler tells INDI that he can do it again, then the dial tone comes back on for a little longer, Kepler says that "they" are going to connect at any moment, but nothing happens. INDI asks if he should do it again, but Kepler is strongly against it.

As the Station Timer counts into July 10th, the livestream interferences become a little more frequent with the [[Station Interruption]] screen and the [[Channel 14]] Demo Channel popping up as the timer reaches closer to 0|0|0|0. There are a lot more frequent beeping noises, as if INDI and Kepler are trying to do something (perhaps trying to contact someone or something). At around the 2 hour mark of the Station Timer, INDI asks Kepler if he ever deciphered [[The Logs]], which Kepler says that he did not. A viewer says that they didn't either and asks if [[The Logs]] were supposed to be deciphered, to which INDI says that [[The Logs]] are not for the viewers, adding that neither INDI nor Kepler have the proper skills or tools to decipher them. Same viewer asks who made [[The Logs]] and INDI responds with "the (blank, redacted), but I'm going to guess that you're not going to get that", which could be one of two things: [[The Eyes]] or The Hands (as in [[TTH]]). Kepler tells INDI not to say who it *actually* is, and INDI obeys.

Some viewers expressed concern about the [[710 Tone|7/10 Tone]] channel getting wiped by [[INDI]] since he has the power to delete things now, but [[INDI]] eventually responds that he can't speak about [[The Logs]] and that they are important to "someone else", but [[Kepler]] stops [[INDI]] before he can elaborate any further.

[[INDI]] tells the chat that he hopes everyone (as in the audience) will all meet again in the future, then Kepler says that "we" are increasing the power, warning [[INDI]] to be ready for it. [[INDI]] says he will be ready, then [[Kepler]] says "cursed thing should not be" (although not specifying on what that "thing" is). After a few more moments, [[Kepler]] switches places with [[INDI]], telling him to "focus on the other things right now" (which is apparently important), to which [[INDI]] agrees. [[Channel 14]]'s Demo Channel comes back on, which [[Kepler]] panics over and in process of his panic accidentally misspells "shit" as "dhit", which get mocked by the viewers. [[Kepler]] yells for [[INDI]] to hurry up before he fixes the problem and puts the [[Station Timer]] back on-screen. Afterwards, [[Kepler]] and [[INDI]] switch back, and then [[Kepler]] tells the audience that [[Travis]] hid something important from him and INDI, which he doesn't know what, apparently [[Travis]] wanted to let everyone know. They increase the power some more, which prompts a weird screechy noise to gradually fade in during the timer, then as the noise reaches its peak, INDI says that they are "piercing through", which would be the last comment made by them as the timer finally reaches zero.

As the countdown ends, viewers receive a prompt from [[INDI]]: 

	Hello Again. You remember my promise, right? All I need is a 5 digit code. Look back into the logs to find it. They will be in descriptions of prime numbered logs.

The viewers quickly solve this, entering **28634** into chat as the input for the code. For the code was initiated successfully, [[INDI]] thanks the viewers for inputting the code, then the antenna logo starts distorting with technical noises in the background, with text "UNKNOWN ARTIFACTS PRESENTED: DISTRIBUTING OTHER DATA" repeating in the background and blurring distortedly. Then the transmission overloads, with the words "I FOUND YOU INDI" appearing on-screen, then everything freezes before cutting to the [[TTH]] logo for a split second and then to the screen test output scene with the color codes 1 (red), 2 (green), and 3 (blue), as well as a stopwatch timer. The videos [[KEY]] and [[lm-1]] are also subsequently posted onto the [[710 Tone|7/10 Tone]] channel.
