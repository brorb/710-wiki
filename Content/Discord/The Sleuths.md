The Sleuths are a group of investigators trying to solve the many mysterious surrounding the [[710 Tone|7/10 Tone]] YouTube channel. 

The group was created by @brorb on 2nd July 2025 after chatters in the then-ongoing 7/10 livestreams voiced interest for a community of "sleuthers".

Our goal is to study and archive all content produced by 7/10, whether it is through solving puzzles or analyzing the videos / community posts for hidden clues. We are also trying to expand the community through spreading the word, with the use of the Discord Server, [YouTube archive channel](https://www.youtube.com/@710ToneArchiveChannel) and community [subreddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/710Tone/)

The archive channel was created after the [[710 Tone|7/10 Tone]] YouTube channel started removing certain videos on July 23rd, 2025.[^1]


[^1]: See [[Community Posts]] and [[710 Tone|7/10 Tone]] for more information.
