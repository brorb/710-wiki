There are many ways you can contribute to [[The Sleuths]]' project! 

Have you found an error? Or wish to add something? Write in the [wiki channel](https://discord.com/channels/1389902002737250314/1392428204098326568)in the Discord server, and we will get it sorted! This is also the place to write if you want to start _writing_ articles! No previous wiki experience required, but if you are familiar with [git](https://git-scm.com/), it is a bonus (though not required).

For those more interested in solving lore-related mysteries, there is a procured list below. Here, you will find open-ended questions and unsolved curiosities related to the 7/10 Tone universe. Any discussion of these mysteries can take place in the comment sections, or in the [forums](https://discord.com/channels/1389902002737250314/1390682153108373547). If you make any progress, we will update the wiki with your findings!

Good luck!
